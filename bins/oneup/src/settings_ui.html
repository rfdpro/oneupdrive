    Keep copies of removed filesFiles deleted or replaced in OneDrive
    
                
    
                    
    Also keep files replaced from OneDriveOneDrive's version history has them anyway
    
                
    
                    
    When removed files don't fit
    
                    
    
                
    
                    
    Delete the oldest copies to make room
    
                
    
                    
    Keep copies for at leastdays
